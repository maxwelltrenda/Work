**Description**:
Bramblebrook is a cozy farming RPG you can play a few minutes at a time.

Grow and harvest crops through the seasons, raise animals, and turn your harvest into crafts and meals. Head
into the wilds to forage, dig in the mine, and fish the river and the cove. Befriend the people of the valley,
read their letters, and follow a story that ends with the Bramble Bell ringing again.

Choose a class like a tabletop hero: fighter, rogue, ranger, wizard, cleric or bard. Roll the dice in story
choices and turn-based battles, and learn perks as you level up.

Make an account to play with others: join a village, pool coins to raise its buildings, and summon village
bosses that everyone fights together over two days. Chat with the valley, follow friends and send messages.

Everything saves on your phone, and online to your account if you make one. No ads, no purchases.
