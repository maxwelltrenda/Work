# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect › App Review Information › Notes, and fill in the demo account.

Bramblebrook Farm is a farming RPG. It plays fully without an account ("Play without an account" on the first
screen). Online features (villages, chat, messages, village bosses) need a free account made in the app with a
username and password; no email is asked for.

Demo account: username and password in the Sign-In Information fields (a ready-made farm with a village).

Safety for user content (guideline 1.2):
- Chat, village posts and messages pass a server-side word filter.
- Every message from another player has a ⋯ button: Report this message, Report this player, Block.
  Profiles have Report and Block too. Blocked players' messages are hidden and they can't message you.
- Players agree to the terms (zero tolerance for abuse) when they create an account.
- Reports reach the developer at once and are reviewed within 24 hours: the message is removed and the player
  muted or banned.

Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): Bag › Settings › Safety & privacy › Delete my account.

The app has no purchases, no ads and no tracking.
