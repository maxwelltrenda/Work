Help

Bramblebrook Farm is made by one person, who reads every message.

Get in touch

In the game, tap the Feedback tab on the edge of the screen (or Bag › Settings › Feedback). Tell us what happened or what you'd like; it arrives straight away, and the answer comes back to your bell 🔔 if you have an account.

@EMAIL@

Someone is being unkind

Tap the ⋯ next to their message and choose Report, or open their profile and tap Report. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. To stop seeing them straight away, tap Block. Unblock them any time in Bag › Settings › Safety & privacy › Blocked players.

Delete your account

Bag › Settings › Safety & privacy › Delete my account, then type your username. Everything online goes straight away; the farm on your device stays. See the privacy policy for details.

Lost password

Accounts have no email, so a password can't be reset. If you're still signed in on any device, your farm is safe there.

Play

Bramblebrook Farm runs in any browser at bramblebrook-farm.vercel.app. Your farm saves on your device, and to your account if you make one.
