Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Bramblebrook Farm is a small game made by an independent developer. It asks for as little as it can: no email, no real name, no location, no contacts, no ads and no tracking.

If you play without an account

Your farm is saved only on your device. Nothing about it is sent to us.

If you send feedback, we receive what you wrote, your farmer's name, which screen you were on, your day and levels, the kind of device and browser you use and its screen size, and your last few taps, messages and errors in the game. We use this only to fix problems.

If you make an account

We keep:

  • Your username and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed) by our database host; nobody can read it, including us.
  • Your farm save, so you can play on any device.
  • Your public profile card: your farmer's name, title, portrait, skill levels and how long you've played.
  • When you were last online. You can hide this from other players in Settings.
  • What you do with other players: who you follow, chat messages, direct messages, your village and its activity, village boss battles, and your notifications.
  • Feedback and reports you send.
  • When you make an account, your network address is turned into a one-way code to stop one place making too many accounts. The code is deleted after a day.
  • If you link the game to Claude, a scrambled copy of your private link.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see your profile card, your online status (unless you hide it), your world chat messages, and, if you're in a village, your village messages and activity.
  • Direct messages are seen only by you and the other player, unless one of you reports a message: then the developer sees it to deal with the report.
  • The developer can look at the game's data to run the game, fix problems and act on reports. We never sell or share your data, and never use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

Accounts and online play are stored with Supabase in the United States. The website is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. If you play the version inside Claude, your farm is also saved to your Claude account under Anthropic's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your account until you delete it. To delete it, open the game and go to Bag › Settings › Safety & privacy › Delete my account. This removes your account, online save, profile, messages, follows and village place straight away. Feedback and reports you sent are kept without your name, so problems can still be fixed.

Children

Bramblebrook Farm has chat with other players, so accounts are not meant for children under 13. Anyone can play without an account, which sends nothing to us.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. The database only lets each player change their own things. Messages pass a word filter, and any player can be reported or blocked.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the game will say so. Questions: see Help.
