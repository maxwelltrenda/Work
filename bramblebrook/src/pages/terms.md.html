Terms and community rules

Last updated November 25, 2026

Bramblebrook Farm is a cozy game. Playing it, and especially making an account, means you agree to these terms.

Community rules

Be kind. There is no tolerance for:

  • bullying, harassment, threats, or telling anyone to hurt themselves;
  • hate speech or slurs about anyone's race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or background;
  • sexual content, or anything sexual involving minors (this is reported to the authorities);
  • sharing anyone's personal details, including your own address, phone number or school;
  • spam, scams, or links meant to trick people;
  • pretending to be the developer or another player;
  • cheating or using bugs in ways that hurt other players. Please report bugs instead.

Messages are filtered automatically. If you see something that breaks the rules, tap the ⋯ next to it, or Report on the player's profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and players who post it are muted or banned. You can also block anyone: you won't see their messages and they can't message you.

Your account

  • An account is for one person. Keep your password somewhere safe: accounts have no email, so a lost password can't be reset.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Bag › Settings.
  • We may mute, ban or remove accounts that break these rules.

What you post

What you write stays yours. By posting it you let us show it to the other players it's meant for, inside the game.

The game

Bramblebrook Farm is free. It is provided as it is, and it will change as it grows. We do our best to keep your farm safe, but we can't promise the game will always be available or free of bugs, and we are not responsible for losses caused by using it, as far as the law allows.
